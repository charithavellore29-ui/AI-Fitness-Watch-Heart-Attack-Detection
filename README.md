
# AI-Integrated Fitness Watches for Early Heart Attack Detection

## Project Overview
An academic research project exploring the integration of Artificial Intelligence into fitness watches to support early identification of potential heart-related warning signs.

## Project Objective
To explore how AI-enabled wearable technology can monitor health indicators, identify unusual patterns and provide timely alerts.

## Key Areas of Study
- AI integration in wearable technology
- Smart fitness watch applications
- Health monitoring and sensor-based tracking
- Early warning and alert systems
- Technology-supported preventive healthcare
